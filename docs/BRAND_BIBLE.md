# Brand bible foundation

## Position

The Tavern is the unofficial home of ALE's bad decisions and strong opinions: a dirty medieval tavern run by armored raccoons beside a strategically illuminated dumpster. It is rough but intentional, funny but not childish, adult without gratuitous vulgarity, loyal, and confidently incompetent.

## Visual territory

Use controlled signals: medieval wood, scratched iron, rust, dirty parchment, ale gold, dumpster green, soot, torchlight, small neon accents, dented tankards, damaged heraldry, paw prints, raccoon-mask shapes, and badly drawn maps. Texture must never impair legibility. Avoid generic esports neon, shields-and-claws clichés, or covering every surface in grime.

## Foundation palette

Issue #2 must validate accessibility and commercial use before adoption. Starting roles: Soot (dark background), Parchment (light surface), Ale Gold (primary accent), Dumpster Green (secondary accent), Ember (limited alert), and clean high-contrast text. Exact values remain an Issue #2 decision.

## Marks required by Issue #2

Primary logo, simplified mark, trash-panda mascot mark, favicon, and social-profile variants. An ALE Tavern badge and any ALE-branded merchandise are **BLOCKED** until the founder confirms authority to use ALE commercially. Every artwork source, font, texture, and icon requires a completed entry in `ASSET_RIGHTS_REGISTER.md`.

## Use rules

- Prefer original silhouettes and improvised heraldry.
- Keep jokes and calls to action readable at mobile sizes.
- Do not imitate Kingshot art direction, UI, logos, characters, screenshots, or marketing layouts.
- Generated-art prompts must not name or reference protected games, characters, artists, franchises, screenshots, or assets. Generated output requires documented human provenance and similarity review against protected and third-party work before approval; generated does not mean rights-cleared.
- Always accompany public brand contexts with the disclaimer defined in `IP_AND_PRIVACY_RULES.md` where practical and legally appropriate.

Issue #2 owns exact colors, typography, marks, examples, and the rights register.

# Character bible foundation

## Creative contract

Issue #3 will create five to seven original recurring trash pandas, select one primary mascot, and coordinate visual rules with Issue #2. Characters must work by silhouette, expression, clothing, accent color, prop, and behavior rather than labels.

## Candidate ensemble

The Innkeeper, War Raccoon, Quartermaster, Drunk Strategist, Diplomat, New Recruit, and Sleeper are provisional archetype prompts, not approved names/designs or real-member portraits. Issue #3 must perform and record an originality and confusing-similarity review before any name, silhouette, costume, prop combination, or character design is approved.

## Required character record

Name; role; personality; appearance; silhouette; clothing/armor; accent; expression range; speech pattern; strength; weakness; recurring prop; running joke; catchphrase; relationships; standard poses; consistency rules; animation complexity; merchandise suitability; three skit concepts; and three merchandise concepts.

## Constraints

- Simple shapes and limited details support repeatable 2D cutout animation and embroidery.
- Distinct silhouettes and accents must remain connected to the Tavern system.
- No Kingshot character resemblance, copied costume, game UI element, or protected artwork.
- No real member's name, likeness, biography, quote, or recognizable incident without documented permission.
- Fictionalized dialogue is never represented as an exact real quote.

Founder approval of the roster and primary mascot is required before commercial or public production.

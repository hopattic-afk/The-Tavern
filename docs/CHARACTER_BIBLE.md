# Character bible — provisional universe

Status: **BLOCKED for public/commercial use** pending founder roster/name/mascot approval, name/trademark screening, independent similarity review, rights clearance, and Sentinel review. These are original fictional Tavern characters, not real ALE members. Issue #3 owns character art; Issue #2 retains the base logo and any future mascot lockup handback.

## System interface

- Seven raccoons use clean silhouettes, two-value facial masks, limited interior detail, and one accent each against Soot/Parchment surfaces.
- Avatar art survives a circular 760/1080 safe area; animation uses separate head, muzzle, torso, upper/lower arms, hands, prop, and optional mouth/eyebrow layers.
- Atkinson/Spectral remain provisional/blocked fonts. The Crooked Tankard mark remains unchanged and independent.
- Standard poses: neutral turnaround, working/prop pose, reaction pose. Standard expressions: neutral, confident, alarmed, delighted, exhausted, suspicious.
- Props may swap only within a character's approved family. Never add protected game UI, logos, costumes, screenshots, or recognizable member details.

## Primary mascot recommendation

**Bramble Bung, the Innkeeper** is recommended, not selected. Weighted score: silhouette 22/25, role breadth 20/20, animation 13/15, merchandise 14/15, expression 9/10, originality risk 8/10, cross-cultural clarity 5/5 = **91/100**. Bramble welcomes visitors, anchors every storyline, avoids combat-first positioning, and complements the non-character tankard mark. Founder approval and equal finalist review remain required.

All seven were scored on the same weights (25/20/15/15/10/10/5): Bramble 91, Pip 84, Vellum 80, Muddle 79, Tansy 78, Rook 76, Drowse 73. Component scores and arithmetic are canonical in `content/characters/characters.json`; validator recomputes every total. Bramble wins breadth and non-combat welcome utility; Pip is the strongest audience-surrogate alternative; the remaining cast have narrower role breadth. This is a recommendation only, not selection.

## Cast records

### Bramble Bung — Innkeeper — Ale Gold

- Personality/role: exhausted, hospitable anchor who converts disasters into house policy. Strength: calm triage. Weakness: accepts responsibility for everyone. Speech: short service-industry certainty.
- Appearance/silhouette: broad pear torso, rolled sleeves, crooked apron, one low shoulder; no armor. Expressions: dry neutral, forced welcome, panic, relief, suspicion, proud exhaustion.
- Prop/joke/catchphrase: dented ledger-tankard; continually starts a damage tab. “Put it on the structural tab.”
- Relationships: restrains Rook, audits Tansy, translates Muddle, relies on Vellum, protects Pip, leaves notes for Drowse.
- Poses/consistency: ledger at left hip; apron patch always right; rounded ears/low center of gravity. Rig: simple sleeves/apron flap, 10 mouth shapes max.
- Concepts — skits: Rook invoices a siege; Muddle's map becomes a menu; Drowse clocks in after closing. Merch: one-color innkeeper bust; structural-tab mug; apron-patch sticker.
- Suitability: strongest mascot/avatar; excellent mug/sticker/shirt; embroidery requires simplified ledger lines.

### Rook Rattleplate — War Raccoon — Ember

- Personality/role: loyal escalator treating inconvenience as siege. Strength: decisive courage. Weakness: no proportional response. Speech: clipped battlefield declarations.
- Appearance/silhouette: tall inverted triangle, oversized mismatched shoulder plate, bent helmet plume. Expressions: vigilant, triumphant, offended, confused, sheepish, battle-joy.
- Prop/joke/catchphrase: tiny wooden practice sword; declares war on furniture. “Form ranks. The chair moved.”
- Relationships: Bramble brakes him; Vellum negotiates around him; Pip idolizes then questions him; Tansy denies his requisitions.
- Poses/consistency: high shoulder plate always viewer-left; sword comically small; no realistic weapon detail. Rig: rigid plate, separate plume, limited torso bend.
- Concepts — skits: war on a stuck door; rally against spilled stew; peace treaty with a stool. Merch: tiny-sword patch; `FORM RANKS` tee; offended helmet sticker.
- Suitability: strong silhouette/shorts; good patch; avoid combat-forward primary mascot positioning.

### Tansy Tallytail — Quartermaster — Dumpster Green

- Personality/role: precise resource guardian with empty personal pockets. Strength: planning. Weakness: hoards broken inventory. Speech: numbered lists and corrective totals.
- Appearance/silhouette: compact rectangle, many pouch tabs, square spectacles, measuring tail ribbon; quilted vest, no armor. Expressions: calculating, appalled, smug, depleted, suspicious, secretly delighted.
- Prop/joke/catchphrase: abacus crate; counts resources that do not exist. “Inventory says no. Again.”
- Relationships: rejects Rook, catches Muddle's arithmetic, trains Pip, spars politely with Bramble.
- Poses/consistency: three visible pouch tabs; square glasses; tail ribbon has two knots. Rig: glasses/abacus separate; minimal pouch motion.
- Concepts — skits: audits an empty cellar; budgets one potato; discovers Rook's emergency spoons. Merch: inventory checklist tee; abacus sticker; `ZERO RESOURCES` tote.
- Suitability: excellent graphic/copy merch; medium animation load; glasses must remain legible small.

### Muddle Quill — Drunk Strategist — Ale Gold

- Personality/role: elaborate lateral thinker whose plans may be upside down. Strength: inventive pattern recognition. Weakness: cannot orient paper. Speech: grand hypotheses and sudden footnotes.
- Appearance/silhouette: lanky S-curve, floppy cap, long scarf, ink-spotted vest. Expressions: inspired, unfocused, certain, startled, scholarly, asleep-standing.
- Prop/joke/catchphrase: rotating parchment map and quill; every map is upside down. “The arrows are emotionally north.”
- Relationships: Tansy checks math; Bramble turns plans into menus; Vellum edits claims; Pip asks fatal reasonable questions.
- Poses/consistency: scarf bends opposite torso; three ink spots; map never shows real coordinates. Rig: scarf/map/quill separate, avoid liquid simulation.
- Concepts — skits: map is dinner order; flawless plan rotated; sixteen-step route across one room. Merch: upside-down map tee; emotional-north poster; quill sticker.
- Suitability: strong skit engine/print composition; embroidery limited to head/quill.

### Vellum Sip — Diplomat — Parchment

- Personality/role: patient mediator carrying emergency refreshments. Strength: reframing conflict. Weakness: negotiates after action. Speech: careful clauses ending in blunt reality.
- Appearance/silhouette: narrow upright oval, long split coat, soft neck ribbon, one chipped cup; light leather shoulder tabs. Expressions: composed, strained smile, alarm, resigned, persuasive, deadpan.
- Prop/joke/catchphrase: chipped peace cup; arrives one attack late. “A productive dialogue would have been earlier.”
- Relationships: translates Rook to others, co-manages with Bramble, edits Muddle, comforts Pip, cannot locate Drowse.
- Poses/consistency: cup always unbroken enough to hold liquid; coat split centered; no bottle/weapon implication. Rig: coat tails/cup separate.
- Concepts — skits: treaty after chair war; mediates potato ownership; drafts apology before Rook finishes. Merch: peace-cup mug; dialogue tee; diplomatic deadpan sticker.
- Suitability: excellent mug/copy; clear animation; light accent needs Soot outline.

### Pip Kindling — New Recruit — Ember

- Personality/role: reasonable audience surrogate immediately over-assigned. Strength: curiosity and adaptability. Weakness: says yes before hearing task. Speech: plain questions followed by worried repetition.
- Appearance/silhouette: small upright triangle, oversized hood, blank wooden badge, rolled task scroll. Expressions: hopeful, confused, overloaded, proud, terrified, determined.
- Prop/joke/catchphrase: ever-growing notification scroll. “Is that still one responsibility?”
- Relationships: mentored by Tansy, protected by Bramble, recruited by Rook, accidentally validates Muddle, wakes Drowse.
- Poses/consistency: hood opening triangular; badge blank; scroll contains abstract lines only. Rig: hood fixed, scroll extendable in simple segments.
- Concepts — skits: one task becomes seventeen; asks what rally means; notification scroll exits frame. Merch: `ONE RESPONSIBILITY` tee; long-scroll sticker; blank-badge patch.
- Suitability: relatable social character; simple merch; never code as child—adult recruit proportions/voice.

### Drowse Coalpaw — Sleeper — Dumpster Green

- Personality/role: serene late arrival who misses every crisis. Strength: unshakable calm. Weakness: timing. Speech: soft one-line questions after conclusions.
- Appearance/silhouette: low horizontal bean, drooping cap, blanket-cape, one soot-dark paw. Expressions: asleep, half-awake, pleasantly confused, startled, satisfied, immediately asleep.
- Prop/joke/catchphrase: silent alarm bell/pillow shield; asks for location afterward. “Did we win the meeting?”
- Relationships: Bramble leaves notes; Pip wakes him; Rook mistakes stillness for discipline; Tansy logs him absent; Vellum reschedules around him.
- Poses/consistency: dark paw always viewer-right; cap droops left; no real coordinates in dialogue/art. Rig: minimal body, eyelids/cap/bell separate.
- Concepts — skits: arrives after victory; sleeps through alarm test; asks coordinates to own chair. Merch: sleeping bean patch; `WIN THE MEETING?` pillow; alarm-bell sticker.
- Suitability: excellent reaction/sticker/embroidery silhouette; narrower story leadership range.

# IP and privacy rules

## Required disclaimer

“An unofficial player-community project. The Tavern and ALE are not affiliated with or endorsed by Kingshot or CenturyGames.”

Use it on the site footer, About/FAQ and relevant store/social contexts. Founder/legal review must confirm final placement and wording; this repository does not provide legal advice.

Mandatory placements are: every website footer, About The Tavern, About ALE, FAQ, incident submission consent screen and confirmation, store landing or outbound commerce disclosure, social profile/link-in-bio landing, and video/post descriptions when the platform permits. Product artwork does not need the disclaimer unless legal review requires it; product descriptions and store policy surfaces do.

## Intellectual property

- Use original Tavern names, writing, characters, compositions, marks, and artwork.
- Never use Kingshot/CenturyGames logos, screenshots, copied characters, game interface, music, text, or protected marketing assets.
- Complete `ASSET_RIGHTS_REGISTER.md` for every third-party or generated font, icon, texture, audio, video, and artwork asset before use.
- Do not assume generated assets are commercially safe; conduct provenance and similarity review.
- “ALE” authority and permitted commercial usage require founder confirmation.

## Personal and alliance information

Do not commit or publish private messages, coordinates, account details, strategy, member identities, recognizable likenesses, or unapproved quotes. Names/likenesses and exact quotes require specific recorded permission. Fictionalized dialogue must be labeled or presented as adaptation, never a real quotation.

## Incident data rules

**NO COLLECTION:** incident intake, test submissions using real information, and storage of member data remain disabled until Issue #8 defines explicit adaptation consent, name/quote permissions, privacy flags, anonymization, rejection criteria, status, withdrawal, retention/deletion, storage region/provider, role-based access, breach response, and the founder records approval of that complete data model. Collect only necessary data. Raw submissions and consent records stay in an approved restricted system; writers receive minimized approved briefs. Remove coordinates, account data, strategy, and sensitive metadata. Do not send personal details to analytics or public logs.

## Review and response

Every asset/content item receives IP/privacy review before production and again before publication. On a credible rights/privacy complaint: unpublish or disable distribution when safe, preserve review evidence privately, notify the founder, investigate, correct/delete as required, and document the resolution.

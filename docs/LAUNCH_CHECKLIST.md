# Launch checklist

Issue #10 owns evidence and the launch decision. A checked item requires a link to current evidence, not assumption.

## Product and site

- [ ] Mobile/desktop, navigation, forms, links, Fourthwall handoff, and email signup tested.
- [ ] Shipping, returns, privacy, terms, contact, disclaimer, SEO, social previews, and custom 404 verified.
- [ ] Accessibility, reduced motion, keyboard, broken-link, performance, analytics, and error-state checks documented.
- [ ] No secrets, private alliance information, prohibited assets, or unreviewed submissions present.

## Merchandise and content

- [ ] Samples received and founder-approved for color, quality, sizing, price, margin, and production expectations.
- [ ] Three videos ready; captions/mobile readability and audio/art rights documented.
- [ ] Social package and every public asset passed IP/privacy review.

## Operations

- [ ] Production configuration, owners, support path, monitoring, backups/evidence, and incident contacts documented.
- [ ] Rollback tested: restore last verified deploy; disable compromised forms/commerce links; pause scheduled posts; notify founder.
- [ ] Critical blockers and noncritical improvements separated.
- [ ] Founder decisions listed separately.

## Approval record

- [ ] Sentinel found no unresolved critical acceptance/privacy/security issue.
- [ ] Founder explicitly approved public website deployment.
- [ ] Founder explicitly approved store opening and products.
- [ ] Founder explicitly approved social publication schedule.

Without all applicable approvals, status is **NO-GO**.

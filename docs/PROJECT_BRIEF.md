# Project brief

## Objective

Build The Tavern into a coherent, commercially usable, unofficial ALE community brand using original medieval trash-panda comedy. The operating system must let contributors produce consistent work without repeatedly requesting basic direction.

## Audience

Primary: adult mobile-strategy gamers and ALE alliance members. Secondary: gamers who understand alliance, resource, rally, and leadership humor without knowing Kingshot.

## Positioning

A polished community tavern for bad plans, strong rallies, original trash-panda characters, and self-aware alliance comedy. Chaotic in voice; disciplined in execution, privacy, accessibility, and commerce.

## Scope

Planned work includes a custom website, Fourthwall merchandise, recurring characters, animated vertical skits, social launches, and consent-based community incident submissions. Foundation work defines constraints and interfaces; it does not create production artwork, launch services, or collect data.

## Launch outcome

A mobile-first public experience that explains the brand, hands commerce to Fourthwall, presents approved content and characters, accepts submissions through an approved privacy-safe system, and links approved social channels. Issue #10 controls launch readiness and the founder makes the final go/no-go decision.

## Non-goals

- Custom checkout, payment storage, tax, printing, fulfillment, or order handling.
- An official Kingshot/CenturyGames experience or replica game interface.
- Publishing private alliance strategy, messages, coordinates, or identities.
- Generic esports branding, child-oriented humor, or unnecessary vulgarity.

## Success principles

Original and recognizable; funny without sacrificing clarity; mobile and accessible; minimal client JavaScript; privacy by design; reversible delivery; evidence-based launch.

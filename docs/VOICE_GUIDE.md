# Voice guide

## Voice

Chaotic, clever, loyal, irreverent, self-aware, adult, and concise. The narrator treats obvious failure as competent tavern administration. Humor punches at plans and situations, not vulnerable people.

Brand status: provisional with Issue #2. Character-specific speech remains Issue #3 work.

## Principles

- Lead with the understandable joke; gamer context is seasoning.
- Use short sentences, concrete nouns, and confident understatement.
- Keep calls to action literal and accessible.
- Avoid slurs, harassment, cruelty, sexual shock, excessive profanity, and claims that fictional dialogue is real.
- Remove private details and write for gamers outside Kingshot.

## Approved working lines

- Bad plans. Strong rallies.
- Dumpster diplomacy.
- Questionable leadership. Unquestionable loyalty.
- The war council has entered the dumpster.
- We had a plan. Then the raccoons arrived.

## Patterns

Good: “The dumpster is not on fire. It is strategically illuminated.”
Avoid: insider-only coordinates, direct mockery of named members, copied game copy, or paragraphs of medieval dialect.

## Channel patterns

- Navigation/buttons: literal first (`Shop the drop`, `Watch Tavern Tales`), with jokes outside the control label.
- Headlines: 2–8 words; one confident failure premise.
- Product copy: name the object, fit/use, material facts, then one short joke. Never obscure care, sizing, shipping, or returns.
- Error copy: state what failed and what to do next; humor is optional and never blames the user.
- Social replies: warm, brief, situation-focused; never confirm private incidents or identities.

## Reusable provisional copy

`Bad plans. Strong rallies.` is the recommended primary line. Secondary lines: `Dumpster diplomacy.` and `Questionable leadership. Unquestionable loyalty.` Do not use more than one slogan on a single small surface. Final primary tagline requires founder approval.

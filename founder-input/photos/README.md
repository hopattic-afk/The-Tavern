# Photo drop

Put photo files in `raw/`, then copy and complete `PHOTO_INPUT_TEMPLATE.md` beside the photo or in `../review-state/`.

Photos are private inspiration by default. Uploading a photo does not authorize copying, publication, merchandise, advertising, use of a recognizable likeness, or use of protected references.

Preferred filenames: `subject-or-mood-yyyy-mm-dd-source.ext`. Avoid names, handles, locations, account IDs or other private details in filenames.

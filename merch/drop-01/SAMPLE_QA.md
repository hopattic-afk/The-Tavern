# Drop 01 sample order and QA gate

No account change, product creation, purchase, or sample order is authorized by this file.

## Before ordering — founder approval required

- [ ] #2 identity and selected marks approved; #3 roster/mascot approved where D03 is used.
- [ ] Rights rows cleared; human name/trademark and confusing-similarity review recorded.
- [ ] Exact Fourthwall product, variant, color, size, print area, file format/profile and dated cost evidence recorded.
- [ ] Final exports validated against vendor requirements; mockups remain placement evidence only.
- [ ] Founder approves product/SKU list, quantities, sample sizes/colors, total item cost, shipping, tax and maximum spend.

## Physical review

Record sample ID, design/version/checksum, order/arrival dates, actual landed cost, reviewer and photos/evidence reference. Review artwork scale/placement, color in documented lighting, contrast, edges, print or stitch quality, garment fit against vendor chart, wash/abrasion after vendor-approved care, mug/adhesion handling only as applicable, packaging damage, production time and shipping time. Mark every SKU pass, revise/resample, or reject.

## Release gate

- [ ] Every offered SKU has a passing physical sample or a founder-recorded rationale scoped to an equivalent verified variant.
- [ ] Founder approves final products, prices, expected margins and production/shipping copy.
- [ ] Sentinel finds no unresolved acceptance, privacy, rights, security or commerce issue.
- [ ] Issue #10 records explicit founder store-opening/public-sale approval.

Failed or changed artwork, vendor product, print method, material or placement requires reassessment and, when material, a new sample. Until every applicable gate passes: **NOT FOR SALE — PROVISIONAL**.

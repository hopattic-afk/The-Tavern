## Voice and hashtags

Write as a confidently confused Tavern operator: short, warm, situation-focused, understandable beyond one game. Never confirm private incidents, identities or membership. Refuse coordinates, account details, strategy and private messages. Escalate privacy/rights complaints to the approved contact path when one exists; do not improvise legal or support promises.

Counts, banned tags and platform rules are `TBD` until live verification. Never use protected-game tags to imply affiliation or chase unrelated trends.

## Review and publication

Every review field remains fail-closed as `pending` or `blocked`; no row is cleared or approved. `REVIEW_LEDGER.md` explains the human review workflow. Moving to `scheduled`, using an account/scheduler, replying, messaging or publishing requires explicit founder approval. Every active unpublished item can be withdrawn; published concerns enter takedown review.

## Pause and rollback

Pause all draft handoffs, invalidate the affected asset/version, remove it from any approved scheduler with founder coordination, preserve redacted evidence, repair and rerun review. For privacy/rights complaints, stop distribution where safe, notify the founder, investigate and document deletion/takedown limits. Do not copy private evidence into Git.
